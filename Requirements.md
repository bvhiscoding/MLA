Submit one complete folder per group on a USB drive. Groups may share a drive to save costs. Put each group’s folder at the top level and name it clearly, for example CLC01_Group07_ProjectName. If you share a drive, include a USB_INDEX.txt listing the groups on it.

Final assessment

Weeks 7–9 labs — 30%
Individual interview — 30%
Group report and reproducibility files — 30%
Individual video — 10%
Organise your USB folder

README.md — List group members and contributions. Give setup instructions, the run order, and the script or notebook that reproduces a main result.
Report/Final_Report.pdf — Explain your question, data and labels, baseline, model choices, evaluation, results, error analysis, and limitations.
Code/ and Results/ — Include scripts or notebooks, dependencies, configurations, split or seed information, and saved outputs needed to check the report. Use relative paths.
Data/ — Include the data you have permission to redistribute. In DATA_README.md, document the source, version, licence, and preparation steps. For restricted data, document how authorised users can obtain it and include an approved sample if available. Remove credentials and any private data you are not authorised to share.
AI_Log/AI_Use_Log.md — Record where AI was used and what you personally checked or corrected. If a language model is part of the pipeline, include the model version, settings, prompts, and relevant saved outputs. State explicitly if you used no AI assistance.
Video/StudentID_Name.mp4 — Include one separate video per member.
Write the report, README, and AI log in English. Use the same final project version in the report, code, results, and videos.

Individual video requirements

Present in English, in your own voice. Briefly introduce the problem and identify your contribution. Show a real example from the final project and explain how it moves from data to a prediction or result. Use a chart, table, or annotated example to explain what the data shows. Demonstrate the project using its interface, notebook, or saved outputs, and interpret one result, error, or limitation.

Export as MP4 at 1080p or higher. Use clear audio and make code, charts, and terminal output readable at normal playback size. Trim long waits and review the exported video. The individual video mark reflects technical explanation, evidence and demonstration, personal contribution, and production quality.

Before handing in the USB, open the folder on another computer. Check that the report opens, every video plays with sound, and the README leads to the code, data instructions, and saved results. On a shared drive, each group should check its folder after the final copy.
